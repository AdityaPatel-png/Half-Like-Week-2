My week 2 project will be a chess set modeled in Fusion360, except the pawns will be able to transform into other pieces when promoting.
I intend to create a pen click style mechanism to make the pawn easily transform with a single click.
Because of their transformation, the only pieces required to fill the board will be the king and a few of each type of pawn.
The majority of the pieces will be 3D printed, other than magnets and sprints.
Unfortunately, due to time constraints, I was only able to make the queen transform from a pawn, while the rest are just regular pieces.
